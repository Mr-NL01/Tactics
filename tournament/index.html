Tactics, 2 vs 2, rotating partners. The schedule is built for you: games spread evenly over the players, nobody on two boards in the same round, and partners and opponents kept fresh for as long as the numbers allow.

Tactics Darts Tournament

Goals are the numbers a team closed: 10 to 20 plus bull, so 12 at most. Both partners get their team's count. Tap a bar to set it; tap the last filled bar again to go one lower. Picking a winner fills in 12 for them if it's still empty.
